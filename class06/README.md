## Communication design

Typography: 

### Optical adjustments

Letters with circular and triangular shapes are made slightly taller than square ones to appear visually similar

On a surface geometrically divided in half (R, P, B, E, etc), the middle arms must align with the optical center, which sits slightly above the original one.

### Type families 

A standard letter has stem thickness equal to 15% of its height

### Letter Spacing

There are not fixed rules for spacing between characters. The space between adjacent vertical strokes should be as large as possible.

### Legibility Typefaces

The most legible Types are the ones where the physiognomic zone of a letter is its upper half, so alphabets with with prominent ascenders and articulated shapes tend to be more legible. Serif fonts with non-intrusive serifs tend to be more readable

### Visibility

* Sans-serif
* regular, medium or bold
* Mix upper and lower case
* Avoid condensed, expanded or italic fonts
* Manually adjust spacing for sizes above 18pt
